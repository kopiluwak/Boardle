Boardle

How to play

Each day there's a new Lichess puzzle. Your opponent has just moved (highlighted). Find the best continuation for both sides by playing moves on the board — they fill the tiles in standard algebraic notation (SAN).

When the row is full, press Submit (or Enter). Tiles change colour:

Rxh2+
Kf1
Rdh8
Nb1
Qf2+

🟩 right move, right slot · 🟨 in the solution, wrong slot · ⬛ not in the solution.

A move must match the SAN exactly (Qxa8# ≠ Qxa8). Mini has 3 moves / 3 tries, Boardle 5/5, Mega 7/7, Maxi 9/9. Undo = Backspace.

Openings tab: beginner White & Black lessons with ideas. Learn (show → hint → recall) and spaced Recall practice — progress is stored separately from daily puzzle stats.

Based on Boardle by Rory Cochrane. Puzzles from the Lichess puzzle database (CC0). Opening ideas are original notes for this personal build (not commercial course text).
